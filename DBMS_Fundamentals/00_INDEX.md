## 1-day plan (≈8–9 hrs)

**Morning (≈4 hrs)**
1. 01 Basics & ER (30 min)
2. 02 Relational model (30 min)
3. 03 SQL (2 hrs). **Write every query yourself.** This is the most tested topic.
4. 04 Normalization (1 hr). Practise finding candidate keys

**Afternoon / evening (≈4–5 hrs)**
1. 05 Transactions (1.5 hr)
2. 06 Indexing (45 min)
3. 07 NoSQL & advanced (45 min)
4. 08 Quick Revision. Answer every question out loud (1 hr)

> Tip: for interviews, give **definition + example + trade-off**. For SQL rounds, practise *"second highest salary"*, *"duplicates"*, *"join types"*, and *"GROUP BY vs HAVING"*.

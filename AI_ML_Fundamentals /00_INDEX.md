## 2-day plan

**Day 1 (≈6 hrs): classical ML**
1. 01 Basics (30 min)
2. 02 Math & Stats (45 min). Focus on Bayes and gradients
3. 03 Preprocessing (45 min)
4. 04 Regression (45 min). Do the gradient-descent step by hand
5. 05 Classification (1.5 hr). Know the pros/cons of every algorithm
6. 06 Unsupervised (45 min)
7. 07 Evaluation (45 min). Compute precision/recall/F1 from a confusion matrix yourself

**Day 2 (≈6 hrs): deep learning, modern AI, and revision**
1. 08 Neural Networks (1.5 hr)
2. 09 NLP / Transformers / LLMs (1.5 hr)
3. 10 Classical AI search (45 min)
4. 11 RL & advanced (45 min)
5. 12 Python libraries (30 min)
6. 13 Quick Revision. Answer every question out loud (1 hr)

> Tip: for interviews, give **definition + intuition + when to use it + trade-off** for each concept. For "compare A vs B" questions, use a table in your head: *what it does, strengths, weaknesses, when to pick it*.
>
> Tip: for OAs, practise the **numericals** (confusion matrix, Bayes, entropy/Gini, k-means step, gradient step, conv output size, minimax). They appear far more than theory you can't compute.

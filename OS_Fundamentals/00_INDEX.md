## 2-day plan

**Day 1 (≈5–6 hrs)**
1. 01 OS Basics (30 min)
2. 02 Processes & Threads (1 hr)
3. 03 CPU Scheduling. Solve every numerical by hand (1.5 hr)
4. 04 Synchronization (1.5 hr)
5. 05 Deadlocks. Do the Banker's example yourself (1 hr)

**Day 2 (≈5–6 hrs)**
1. 06 Memory Management. Practise the address-translation math (1.5 hr)
2. 07 Virtual Memory. Solve the page-replacement strings by hand (1.5 hr)
3. 08 File Systems (45 min)
4. 09 Disk Scheduling. Solve the numericals (45 min)
5. 10 Quick Revision. Answer every question out loud (1 hr)

> Tip: for interviews, be ready to give the **definition + a real-world example + a trade-off** for each concept. That combination is what interviewers look for.

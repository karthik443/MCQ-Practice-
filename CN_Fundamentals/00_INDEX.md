## 1-day plan (≈8–9 hrs)

**Morning (≈4.5 hrs)**
1. 01 Basics & models (45 min). **OSI layers and devices by heart**
2. 02 Data link (1.5 hr). Do the CRC, Hamming and sliding-window numericals by hand
3. 03 IP & subnetting (1.5 hr). **Solve 10 subnetting problems**
4. 04 Routing & protocols (45 min)

**Afternoon / evening (≈4 hrs)**
1. 05 Transport (1.5 hr). **TCP handshake, congestion control**
2. 06 Application layer (1 hr). **DNS, HTTP, URL journey**
3. 07 Security & misc (30 min)
4. 08 Quick Revision. Answer every question out loud (1 hr)

> Tip: interviewers love **"What happens when you type google.com in the browser?"**, **TCP vs UDP**, **OSI layers**, **HTTP vs HTTPS**, and **3-way handshake**. Be ready to walk through each end to end.

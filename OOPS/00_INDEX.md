# OOPS — Placement Revision Kit

Topic-wise notes for OOP interviews and MCQ rounds (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Amazon, Microsoft, and similar).
Examples are in **Java and C++** (Python notes where they differ). Each file has **concepts → examples → common interview questions → MCQ traps → practice MCQs**.

## 1-day plan (≈6–7 hrs)

**Morning (≈3.5 hrs)**
1. 01 Basics & four pillars (1 hr)
2. 02 Inheritance & polymorphism (1.5 hr). **Overloading vs overriding and virtual functions are asked every time**
3. 03 Advanced concepts (1 hr)

**Afternoon (≈3 hrs)**
1. 04 SOLID & design patterns (1 hr). Be able to write Singleton and Factory
2. 05 Code-output practice (1 hr). **Predict the output before reading the answer**
3. 06 Quick Revision. Answer every question out loud (1 hr)

> Tip: for interviews, always explain with a **real-life example**: *Car* (class) → *my Tesla* (object); *ATM* (abstraction); *Bank account's private balance* (encapsulation); *Shape → Circle/Rectangle* (polymorphism).
